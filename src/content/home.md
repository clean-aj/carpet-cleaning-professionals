Carpets take a lot of punishment: everyday foot traffic, spills, pets and muddy shoes. Over time, dirt works deep into the fibres, making carpets look tired and dull and trapping allergens and odours.

Our professional cleaning removes that deep-down dirt and restores the colour and softness of your carpets, rugs and upholstery. It also helps them last longer, saving you the cost of replacing them.
