## About Bournemouth

Bournemouth is a coastal town in Dorset, known for its long sandy beaches, its pier and its wooded chines that run down to the sea. Together with Christchurch and Poole, it forms the Bournemouth, Christchurch and Poole council area. Its homes include Victorian villas, seafront apartments and family houses, alongside many holiday lets and student homes.

For your carpets, coastal living means sand, busy summers and quick turnarounds between guests. A professional deep clean keeps them looking good, smelling fresh and ready for the next visitor.
