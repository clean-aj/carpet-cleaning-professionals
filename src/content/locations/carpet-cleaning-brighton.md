## About Brighton

Brighton is a seaside city on the south coast of England and, together with Hove, forms the city of Brighton and Hove in East Sussex. It is famous for the Royal Pavilion, Brighton Palace Pier and the narrow streets of The Lanes. Its homes include Regency terraces, Victorian conversions and modern seafront flats, alongside a large number of student lets and holiday lets.

For your carpets, coastal living means fine sand, salty air and busy rental turnovers. A professional deep clean protects your carpets, impresses your guests and helps your tenants leave on good terms.
