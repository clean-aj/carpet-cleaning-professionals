## About Guildford

Guildford is a historic town in Surrey, set on the River Wey at the edge of the Surrey Hills. It is known for its cobbled High Street, Guildford Cathedral and the University of Surrey. Its homes include period cottages, Victorian and Edwardian houses and modern family homes, alongside student lets and busy town-centre offices.

For your carpets, that means a mix of delicate natural fibres, hard-working family carpets and rentals that need a refresh between tenants. Each one gets a cleaning method chosen to suit it.
