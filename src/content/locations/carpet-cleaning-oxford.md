## About Oxford

Oxford is a city in Oxfordshire, famous as home to the University of Oxford, the oldest university in the English-speaking world. It sits on the River Thames and the River Cherwell, and its skyline of college spires gave it the name "the city of dreaming spires". Its homes include historic townhouses, Victorian terraces and modern family houses, alongside a large number of student and rental properties.

For your carpets, that means delicate period fibres, hard-working family carpets and frequent tenancy changeovers. Each one gets a cleaning method chosen to suit it.
