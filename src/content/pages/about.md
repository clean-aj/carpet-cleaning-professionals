## Who We Are

Your home deserves cleaners who care about it as much as you do. We're a team of local, experienced and trustworthy carpet and upholstery specialists, cleaning homes and businesses right across the South of England.

We specialise in carpets, rugs, upholstery and curtains, with expert stain removal and hot water extraction at the heart of everything we do.

## How We Started

We began as university graduates cleaning carpets part-time in our local area. Our eye for detail, perfectionist work ethic and love of a job well done soon turned it into a full business. Today, we look after residential and commercial properties across the South of England.

## What Sets Us Apart

- **State-of-the-art equipment.** Our hot water extraction machines carry out the cleaning method carpet manufacturers recommend, paired with top-of-the-range solutions that lift even the toughest stains.
- **Fully trained technicians.** Every technician receives continuous in-house training, plus training with the National Carpet Cleaners Association (NCCA), the UK's leading carpet cleaning trade association.
- **Perfectionists at heart.** We always look for ways to go the extra mile, so your property is left looking, feeling and smelling completely refreshed.

## How We Work

- **We assess first.** We check your carpet type, its condition and any stains, so we use the right method and solution.
- **We protect your home.** We move light furniture, protect skirting boards and door frames, and leave everything as we found it.
- **We clean thoroughly.** Pre-treatment, agitation and powerful hot water extraction lift out deep-down dirt.
- **We check with you.** We walk through the finished job with you before we leave.
