## Who we are

Carpet Cleaning Professionals is a specialist carpet, rug and upholstery cleaning company. We work for homeowners, landlords, letting agents and businesses, and every job gets the same care and attention to detail.

## How we work

- **We assess first.** We check the carpet type, condition and any stains before we start, so we use the right method and solution.
- **We protect your home.** We move light furniture, protect skirting boards and door frames, and leave everything as we found it.
- **We clean thoroughly.** Pre-treatment, agitation and powerful hot water extraction lift out deep-down dirt.
- **We check with you.** We walk through the finished job with you before we leave.

## Our promise

If you're not happy with any part of the clean, tell us within 48 hours and we'll come back and put it right.
