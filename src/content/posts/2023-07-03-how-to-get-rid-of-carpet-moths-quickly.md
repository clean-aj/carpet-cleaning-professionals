### How to Get Rid of Carpet Moths the Easy Way (Before You Call the Pros)

First off, you need to find out where the moths are hanging around. Most of the time, they will hang around on walls as the larvae pupate. This means that they might not be immediately visible on the carpet, but instead on the darker parts of your wall around the carpet itself. Look out for them, and if you spot even one carpet moth then it is time to take immediate action.

The clearest sign that you have carpet moths – even if you cannot see the moth itself – is the sign of damaged parts of your carpet. You might find bits of your carpet look a touch bare, or you have come across some strange holes within the carpet. Your job should now involve vacuuming underneath all of your furniture in the room with the carpet and along the baseboards as tightly as you can. 

Give the carpet a serious clean over with your vacuum, making sure that you are extremely thorough in the corners. You can then place some carpet moth traps down, which you can buy online. These might help to stop any additional carpet moths from appearing and placing their eggs and larvae into the carpet. Given the larvae is the aspect that does the damage, using traps can stop the moth from laying eggs in the first place.

You should then consider giving your carpet a thorough spray and clean. You can use a carpet moth killer that you find online, but even just giving your carpet a continuous clean with the usual methods you use should be a good enough starting point. The main thing to do is give the carpet the heavy, thorough vacuuming that we mentioned.

Start here, and you should hopefully see a reduction or full-on end of the moth problem. If they are still present, you should probably think about hiring a professional.
