### When Should You Have Your Upholstery Deep Cleaned?

**The general consensus would be that you should aim for a six-monthly professional cleaning for your upholstery**. However, it is also fair to note that leaving it to a once-per-year process is also deemed enough. This is a good enough timeframe to ensure that your upholstery gets a thorough and fair cleaning. At the same time, though, you should look to ensure that you clean as often as your budget allows.

Once per six months is the preferred option. This tends to help clean up the material and leave it looking, feeling and smelling fresh. At the same time, though, once per year is enough to help keep the upholstery away from the dump. Once per year, though, should be your absolute minimum timeframe. This allows you to give your sofa a proper clean and to make sure that it looks good for longer, but a six-monthly cleaning schedule is likely to work out more in general. 

One thing to note, though, is that you should be giving your upholstery a [surface-level clean](https://www.bissell.eu/tips/carpet-cleaning-buying-guide.html) at least once per month. Ideally, aim for every 2-3 weeks. If you happen to spill something on your upholstery, make sure that you take immediate action to have it cleaned and cared for in the best way possible.

This might involve having to get the covers cleaned professionally. Still, it ensures that you are not going to be looking at upholstery that is damaged to a borderline irreversible level. The sooner you act, the better!
