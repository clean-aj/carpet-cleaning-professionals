## Use This Quick and Simple Method for Treating Carpet Moths

If you have moths at home, then it is not something that you should ignore. While moths can seem harmless, outside of their less-than-appealing nature, they are something that you need to deal with. Why? Because moths have a nasty habit of eating through our clothing and carpet fibres. Leave a group of moths to feast, and you will soon find that your finest jackets and your best carpets and rugs are devoured!

As such, you should take immediate action if you ever need to deal with carpet moths. Thankfully, **treating carpet moths** is just not as difficult as it once was. By being thorough with your home cleaning and care, you can prevent carpet moths from laying nests in your home.

### How to Go About Treating Carpet Moths

The first thing that you need to do is give your carpet and upholstery the most thorough of cleans. This means taking the time to go around the entire carpet with a vacuum cleaner. Take multiple runs over every section – do not assume that a quick, surface-level clean is enough. Take your time and do it slowly, and you can start to see a big difference in the quality of your carpet already.

As you vacuum, be sure to pay special attention to places like underneath TV units and furniture. Carpet moths tend to reside in darker parts of your home, so you should not assume that if you cannot see them they are not present. Get under upholstery and furniture and you can make sure that you stand a better chance of removing these horrible critters.

Next, be sure to be really thorough as you run your vacuum cleaner along your baseboards. The more that you can do this, the more likely you are to catch the moths. Also, look out for darker areas of your walls – perhaps where shadows loom prominently. Moths tend to use these for hiding and for somewhere to rest; if you see a moth, get rid of it.

Now, you should probably also consider using an anti-moth spray that you can buy online on your carpet. Spray this liberally as per the instructions on the packaging. This will help you to make sure that you can further reduce the risk of these moths from becoming a mainstay in your home.

Treating carpet moths really is about being consistent with cleaning. If you start by getting rid of things that build up like dirt, dust, and debris, you reduce the risk of carpet moths ever settling. When your carpet is a haven of such things, though, you make it easier for the moths to wish to come along and settle down. Take preventative action with some regular carpet cleaning, though, and you can alleviate the issue before it gets worse.
