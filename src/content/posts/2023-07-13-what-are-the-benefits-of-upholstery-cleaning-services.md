## Find Out Why Hot Water Extraction Beats Any DIY Steam Cleaning

When it comes to cleaning your home, there are many parts of cleaning that can seem downright confusing. One of the most common places that people come unstuck is with their furniture and upholstery. Naturally, the cost of upholstery means that we want to do everything we can to avoid damaging it. As such, you might want to avoid DIY cleaning in case you make matters worse!

With that in mind, you might also consider hiring a professional upholstery cleaning service to do the job for you. What are the main benefits of leaving your upholstery cleaning to professionals? Let’s take a quick look.

### The Benefits of Upholstery Cleaning Services

###### Avoid Mishaps
One of the most common benefits of a professional service for cleaning upholstery on your behalf is their attention to detail. They will ensure that they only use the right cleaning materials and methods. This results in a cleaning service that makes your sofas and upholstery look better without the risk of using the wrong kind of cleaning methods or cleaning ingredients.

###### You Are Insured
Almost any upholstery cleaning service worth hiring will come with insurance. This means that in the worst-case scenario where something does go wrong, you are protected from the resultant damage. If you ruin your own sofa, though, good luck getting your house insurance to [pay out!](https://myhome.co.uk/upholstery-cleaning/) By contrast, upholstery cleaning pros are insured.

###### Better Cleaning Methods
Another positive aspect of hiring the pros is that they can make sure that your upholstery is given a deep, thorough clean. If you clean yourself you likely stop after a vacuum and perhaps a quick steam cleaning. However, even the best steam cleaning cannot match the use of **hot water extraction**.

This method is one of the most powerful cleaning methods around, getting right down to the deepest part of the upholstery fibres. This makes it easier to clean the dirtiest parts of the couch. The deep-down layers of dirt are the parts that make your upholstery look dirty. Steam cleaning often does not get deep enough; hot water extraction does.

###### Prompt & Time Saving
Hiring the pros to do the job also means that you do not have to invest your hard-earned free time cleaning sofas and upholstery. This can be a job that winds up taking most of your weekend to get right. By contrast, hiring the pros means that they do the job for you. They can even do the job while you are busy/at work, meaning that you can come home to a nice, gleaming upholstery set that looks good as new.

###### Value for Money
You go out and you buy a steam cleaner. You then need to buy cleaning materials, and then commit the aforementioned time to the cleaning process. That costs you a lot of time and money. By hiring a professional, though, you are much more likely to get value for your money without having to lift a finger yourself. Does that not sound preferable to cleaning upholstery on your rare days off?
