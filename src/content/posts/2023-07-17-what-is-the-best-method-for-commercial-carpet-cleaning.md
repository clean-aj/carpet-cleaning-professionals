### The Benefits of Hot Water Extraction

- One of the main reasons to go about using hot water extraction is the fact that it gets so deep into the carpet. As we walk on top of our carpets, we push the dirt and dust that is building up to the very bottom of the carpet fibres. Over time, this makes the carpet look dirty regardless of cleaning.
- At the same time, this means that the dirt starts to impact the carpet at its very base layer. By using hot water extraction during commercial carpet cleaning, though, we make sure that we get as deep as possible. This helps to loosen up all of that dirt that is caked at the bottom of your carpet.
- Once the pressure and the detergent combination of hot water extraction get to work, this breaks up all of this dirt. Then, it can be easily cleaned up and removed with the use of a high-power vacuum cleaning device. This will help to loosen the dirt, freshen it up for removal, and provide deep cleaning.
- The end result is a carpet that looks and feels fantastic and has its old colour and energy restored to full functionality. On top of that, though, the hot water extraction method also breaks up every aspect of the dirt so that it is removed entirely. Now, nothing lingers at the bottom of your carpet.
- Why does this matter? Because it ensures that the carpet is going to look cleaner for longer. Normal commercial carpet cleaning usually only really deals with the surface and mid-layers of the carpet. With hot water extraction, though, we get right to the bottom to really clean that carpet up
