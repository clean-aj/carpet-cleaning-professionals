### How to Clean a Velvet Sofa Properly

As noted by home care specialists [Homes and Gardens](https://www.homesandgardens.com/interior-design/living-rooms/how-to-clean-a-velvet-couch), velvet is usually misconstrued as being high-maintenance. This is not the case. Yes, velvet requires more effort than typical fabric. But when compared to something like leather, there is not much more work needed to manage a velvet sofa. However, the difference with velvet is that a single mistake can cause irreparable damage. 

To start off, you always give your [velvet sofa a thorough vacuum clean](https://www.amazon.co.uk/s?k=cleaner+for+velvet+sofa&adgrpid=133979633925&hvadid=605316179946&hvdev=c&hvlocphy=1007116&hvnetw=g&hvqmt=e&hvrand=11521566833750420051&hvtargid=kwd-1153108351911&hydadcr=24863_2248279&tag=googhydr-21&ref=pd_sl_43gc1qvj6b_e). You start off by being as thorough as you can, getting in between all of the different sections and areas of the sofa as closely as you can. Once you do this, you should look out for any stains that might have appeared on the sofa.

Take a small combination of hot water and dish soap, and then mix this together. Put this in a bowl, and then get a lint-free cloth – a new cloth, basically – and start blotting any stains that you might notice. This should help to remove any damage that has been done to the velvet. You might need to repeat this for more troublesome stains, but this should generally work.

Next, you need to steam clean the velvet. Velvet is infamous for creasing, so you want to steam clean it so that you can lift up as much of the creases as possible. Once you steam clean the velvet – make sure you use the temperature suggested on the care tag of your velvet sofa – you need to let it dry. Finally, give that velvet a brush to help get rid of any final creases, and you *should* be left with a lovely, clean velvet sofa once again!

The biggest problem that you can have with velvet is two-fold: failing to let it dry when you have cleaned it, which leads to a build-up of dampness and odours, and placing the velvet sofa in direct sunlight. UV radiation will dull the lustre and charm of your velvet sofa, so keep it out of the position of the sun. 

Keep these two factors in mind and use the above cleaning, and you should have nothing to worry about.
