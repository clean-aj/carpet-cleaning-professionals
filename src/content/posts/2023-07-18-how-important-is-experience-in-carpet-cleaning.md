## Does Experience Matter When Hiring Carpet Cleaning Pros?

If you are going to go down the route of having your carpets cleaned by a professional, you want someone who knows what they are doing. Sure, an experienced carpet cleaning company might have a higher price range than the cheap novice in your local area, but the results will almost always speak for themselves – and not in a good way!

When you hire a [carpet cleaning company](https://www.indeed.com/q-Carpet-Cleaning-Technician), how important is experience? In a word, very!

Experience is so important in carpet cleaning. This is because experience means that you know the company has been in the industry for a while. You do not want to take the risk of hiring someone who has anything less than five years of experience. This means they have dealt with many carpets and have done a good enough job that people hire them and/or recommend them to others.

Yes, you will pay more for an experienced service like the **Carpet Cleaning Pros**. But, with competitive pricing and a standard of work that speaks for itself, you will get far more value for money for your investment. Hire the ‘cheap’ novice from an online directory, and you might find yourself cursing your luck. 

Why? Because they might make a serious mistake. They might use a cleaning solution that should not be used on the kind of carpet you have. They might be overly strenuous in their cleaning and do more damage than good. Or, they might vanish after the first day of work, never to be seen again.

### Experience Is Worth Paying for in Carpet Cleaning

Make no mistake, carpet cleaning has to be carried out by people who you can trust to know what they are doing. The cost of an incorrect carpet cleaning can be massive. It can leave you with a lot of wasted money and a damaged carpet that cannot be fixed. All it takes is one incorrect cleaning method or one incorrect use of cleaning material, and you can be looking at a carpet that may as well be ripped up and replaced.

That is not what you want when you hire a carpet cleaner. Experienced companies arrive with a solid body of work and proof that they know what they are doing. This can give you that much-needed peace of mind that the company you have hired will do the job correctly. If nothing else, experienced companies have insurance and can provide extra protection through the promise of support should something go awry.

Experience, then, is not something that you should ignore when hiring carpet cleaning pros. Anything under five years of experience might mean you should look for someone else with that level of experience. Yes, you might pay a little more for the cleaning, but you will not pay for a poorly cleaned carpet down the line!
