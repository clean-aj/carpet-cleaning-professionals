## What Sofa Fabrics Are Easiest for Professional Sofa Cleaners to Clean?

When you invest in a fabric sofa, it stands to reason that you want something that is easy to keep in good condition. As you might have already found out to your cost, though, some fabrics are easier to keep clean than others. If you have a nice, easy-to-clean sofa fabric, you can find it easy to keep on top of sofa cleaning with some DIY care. For more arduous fabrics, though, that is when the problems begin!

Sofa fabrics are not the same: some are going to be very easy to look after. Others require specialist care, specific cleaning methods, and a lot of patience. To help you avoid buying a sofa that is a nightmare to clean, you should keep an eye out for the following types of sofa fabric.

### Which Sofa Fabrics Clean Easiest?

###### Fibre
Most sofas today are made using a microfiber material that is durable and low-maintenance when it comes to cleaning. This is a simple textile to look after, and most of the time, it simply needs you to clean it down with a damp towel and a bucket of soapy water. 

###### Vinyl
Vinyl is increasingly common when it comes to sofa fabric, and it is great for those who are looking for a casual, modern-looking fabric style. It is a great material for upholstery as it is easily cleaned and disinfected. Most of the time, you need to wipe it down with a damp cloth and some disinfectant. This is one of the best solutions for those looking for low-maintenance sofa fabrics.

###### Velvet
Velvet is a toughie to clean in that you need to be very careful about things like creases. However, velvet is easy to clean in that it simply needs you to use a steam cleaner to get rid of almost any impact that might appear on a velvet sofa. 

###### Leather
Lastly, think about leather. While it can be hard to clean in some ways, in that you do not want to use the wrong kind of cleaning agent, it is very simple to clean. For the most part, a damp cloth with some disinfectant can be enough to give your sofa a quick wipe-down to ensure that it still looks great. Leather is quite a resistant material anyway, so keeping it clean can be surprisingly simple.

As ever, though, make sure that you read the care tag of any sofa you are about to clean. This might help you avoid using something that you really should avoid on that particular surface!
