## What Is the Best Cleaning Solution for Commercial Carpet Cleaning?

If you want to look after a carpet in a commercial setting, then it is imperative that it is looked after properly. Carpets need to be given due attention and care to make sure that they retain the look and lustre that one would expect. 

As we all know, over time, carpets can become worn-out and damaged looking. From stains and spills to constant wear and tear, it is important you keep your carpet clean.

However, if you intend to use **commercial carpet cleaning**, you need someone you can trust. You need to know what solutions are going to be used. At Carpet Cleaning Pros, we use a wide range of different carpet cleaning services. For commercial carpet cleaning, though, we always use one method.

That method is known as hot water extraction. It is one of the most effective carpet cleaning methods in the world today. Not only does it make keeping your carpet fresh and clean simple, but it creates long-lasting results. Let’s take a look at why we use hot water extraction and why it is the best solution we have.

### Why Do We Use Hot Water Extraction as Our Cleaning Solution?

Almost every cleaning method has some form of drawback – except hot water extraction. When used by the right professionals, this thorough cleaning method cleans the carpet down to the deepest sections. The carpet is made to look clean and fresh simply because of the pressure that it provides.

The mixture of hot water with pressure and detergent is a deadly mixture for dirt, debris and other build-ups of mess. This helps to tear through the carpet’s deepest fibres, breaking up all of that dirt that has been left to build up. Since this cleans out the carpet entirely, this makes the carpet itself look much fresher. Many carpet cleaning methods only focus on the surface layer: hot water extraction does not.

Instead, this goes extremely deep to help lift out all of the mess and debris that is building up at the bottom. This then makes it easy for our carpet cleaning pros to go over the top of the carpet and lift up all of the mess. Instead of simply making the top layer of the carpet look fresh, this makes the whole carpet look great.

This means that results last for much longer as it takes a lot longer for that dirt build-up to formulate in the first place. At the same time, though, hot water extraction is proven to be extremely effective for getting rid of stains and other problems with your carpet. If you are looking for a commercial carpet cleaning service that really delivers lasting results for the long term, start with hot water extraction.
