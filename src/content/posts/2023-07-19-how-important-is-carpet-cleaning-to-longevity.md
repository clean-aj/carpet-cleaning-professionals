### Does Carpet Cleaning Make Your Carpet Last Longer?

In a word, yes!

Carpet cleaning will always help to make your carpet last longer. The fabrics will stay in better condition, the carpet is not likely to become as physically damaged, and it will simply look fresher once you finish. That trio of benefits are just some of the main reasons you should have your [carpet cleaned](https://homedaily.co.uk/best-carpet-cleaners/). 

If you want to make a carpet last more than a few years, it must be cleaned regularly. Weekly vacuum cleaning and then monthly deep cleaning should be the DIY option. For professional cleaning, though, you should look at an annual – or twice-annual, if possible – clean. If you have recently had a big party or social engagement at home, or you have been decorating in a room with a carpet, it might be wise to arrange a post-party/décor clean-up. This is going to help keep the carpet fresher.

Another benefit of regular carpet cleaning is ensuring that things like stains and spills do not overstay their welcome. The quicker you can deal with stains and spills, the more likely you can get rid of them permanently. This stops things like bad odours from building up within the carpet.

In general, though, carpet cleaning can make a carpet last many years. Regular cleaning can mean that you only need to change your carpet when you decide you want a new style. Without regular vacuuming and semi-regular deep cleaning, though, you run the risk of having to replace your carpet every few years. Who wants to do that?
