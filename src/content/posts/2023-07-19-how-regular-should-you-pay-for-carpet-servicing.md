## How Often Is Carpet Servicing London Recommended?

Carpets are something that we can find hard to find the time to clean. The time-consuming aspect of carpet cleaning means that for busy Londoners it can feel easy to just keep putting off the carpet cleaning that is required. However, you might find that over time this begins to show your carpet up in all of the wrong ways. From how dirty the carpet looks to the presence of stains, your carpet will begin to look like it needs a good old-fashioned clean!

With that being the case, you might think about hiring professionals to do the job for you. Today, carpet **servicing London** is very popular given how busy life is in the capital. How often, though, should you be looking to have your carpet serviced by a cleaning professional? Let’s take a look at the recommended timeframe for carpet servicing today.

### When Should You Have Your Carpets Serviced?

Typically, you should be looking to have a thorough carpet cleaning session carried out once every six months. At the very least, you should be looking for a once-per-year major carpet clean-up. This will involve the use of ‘deep cleaning’ methods such as steam cleaning and/or hot water extraction. This is very important to book because these services are what will keep your carpet looking great.

These thorough cleaning sessions are important to book on a schedule so that you do not forget. Our recommended time is to have your carpet cleaned at the beginning of summer, and then at the beginning of the New Year. This gives you two carpet cleaning sessions per year that help to really bring your carpet back to the original condition that you would expect.

At the same time, though, you might find that having your carpets serviced twice per year is not enough. If you are someone who has a lot of foot traffic in your home, or you are perhaps dealing with a carpet in a commercial setting, you might wish to go for a seasonal clean – this would mean three or four cleans per year.

Deep cleaning matters because it helps to break up much of the dirt and debris that has built up on your carpet. While vacuuming your carpet on your own can get rid of a lot of the base mess that has built up, it will not get rid of everything. As such, it pays to invest in a service that can do the ‘heavy lifting’ for you.

So, you should absolutely look to arrange at least one [deep cleaning service in London](https://www.checkatrade.com/blog/cost-guides/carpet-cleaning-cost/) per year. Ideally, though, you want to stretch this to around two services. For those in a commercial setting, though, consider more regularly such as once every 3-4 months to get the most out of your carpet.
