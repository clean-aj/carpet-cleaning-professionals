## Find Out the Most Reliable Carpet Cleaning Services to Hire Today

When it comes to carpet cleaning, one of the most challenging aspects of the process is finding out what solution to use. Most forms of carpet cleaning are pretty thorough, but some are more effective than others. If you are looking for the deepest clean, for example, you are looking for hot water extraction.

Today, hot water extraction is recommended by most **carpet cleaning services**. Since it breaks down all of the dirt building up within the carpet, you can look forward to making your carpet look and smell fresh from the deepest layer upwards. Other carpet cleaning forms only focus on the top and mid-layer of carpet fibres. With hot water extraction, though, you are guaranteed a deep clean of your carpet.

The best way to get a carpet free from dirt build-up and musky smells is to go for hot water extraction. When paired with professional usage and with the right detergent solution, hot water extraction can make a massive difference to even the dirtiest carpets!

### How Often Should You Invest in Hot Water Extraction?

The best time to think about going for a hot water extraction process to deep clean your carpet would be around the mid-way point of the year. Ideally, you want to start the year with a hot water extraction clean and then go for another hot water extraction service around the late summer-early autumn time. 

This gives you a carpet that is cleaned to a high standard twice per year, and it means that you do not have to worry about dirt build-ups that might impact things like air quality, room odours and allergies. At the very least, you should be looking to use a hot water extraction service around once per year – this should be enough to keep most carpets in a good enough condition that you can be happy with them.

However, for carpets in high-traffic areas of your home, like living rooms, and especially for commercial enterprises, you might benefit from a more regular hot water extraction service. Carpets in offices and shops, for example, would benefit from seasonal hot water extraction just because of how likely they are to get damaged as the year goes on.

With this in mind, you should find it much easier to go ahead and have your carpet cleaned to a high and suitable standard. Generally, then, consider hot water extraction if you are looking for the [most effective way](https://www.which.co.uk/reviews/carpets/article/how-to-clean-a-carpet-ajwSx0s1d9ux) to get your carpet cleaned and looking as good as it should. An annual clean is enough, but you should be looking at twice-per-year cleans or, better yet, seasonal cleans for the best results.
