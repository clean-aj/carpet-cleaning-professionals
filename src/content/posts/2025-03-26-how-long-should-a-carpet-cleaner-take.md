## Find Out How Long Your Carpet Cleaner Service Should Take to Finish

If you hire a carpet cleaning service, then it is likely that you will want to know how long the job should take. Most people hire carpet cleaners to come and do the job when they are at work so that they can come home to a fresh and clean carpet. However, every single job varies in how long the job takes.

The main thing that a **carpet cleaner service** will focus on is the size of your carpet. This can help them develop a general ballpark figure for how long the cleaning should take. However, the type of carpet you have, as well as the kind of cleaning that is needed, will also adjust how long the cleaning takes. For example, a quick vacuum and tidy-up of your carpet will take longer than steam cleaning.

Hot water extraction will take even longer than steam cleaning but provides the best results and finish. That being said, though, other factors come into determining how long the job will take. For example, is the carpet already damaged? Will any obstacles need to be navigated around like furniture?

Speaking to your carpet cleaning professional before you confirm the job should give you a good idea of how long it should take. This means that you can then prepare for the length of time that the cleaning should take on average.

Most carpet cleaners will look to clean a typical room in an hour or two at the most, though more complex layouts and larger rooms might require more time than this.

### Get a Timeframe Upfront from Any Carpet Cleaner

Since most carpet cleaning companies charge for their services per hour, you want to be certain that they can do the job to a suitable standard. As such, you should make it clear that they can do the job in a given timeframe. During their initial appointment and consultation with you, you should be able to find out a lot about the service and how long they think it should take.

Remember, though, that while a carpet cleaner can provide an upfront time, things can change during the job. Issues might come up during cleaning, or the carpet might need a [more thorough cleaning](https://www.bisselldirect.co.uk/tips/post/5-answers-to-whether-you-should-deep-clean-carpets) than was initially expected. This can prolong the length of the cleaning service and thus mean it costs more.

If you get a price and timeframe for completion up front, though, it should be easier for you to organise everything around the rest of the project. Generally, this can make it easier for you to understand what kind of costs will be involved and how long you should realistically give a cleaner.
