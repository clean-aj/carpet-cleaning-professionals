Acting quickly makes a big difference with carpet stains. These are the first steps to take before a stain has a chance to set.

## The golden rules

1. **Blot, don't rub.** Rubbing spreads the stain and damages the fibres.
2. **Work from the outside in** so the stain doesn't spread.
3. **Test first.** Try any product on a hidden area before you use it.
4. **Don't over-wet.** Too much water can soak the underlay and cause odours.

## Red wine, tea and coffee

Blot up as much liquid as you can with a clean white cloth. Dab with cool water, blot again, and repeat until no more colour comes out.

## Mud

Let it dry completely, then break it up and vacuum it away. Treat any mark that's left with a little cool water and blot.

## Pet accidents

Blot thoroughly, then use an enzyme-based cleaner made for pet stains. Household cleaners that contain ammonia can smell like urine to pets and encourage them to go in the same spot again.

## When to call a professional

If a stain is large, old, or has already been treated with a product that didn't work, it's best to call a professional. The wrong treatment can set a stain permanently.
