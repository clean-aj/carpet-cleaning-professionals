## Our carpet cleaning process

1. **Inspection:** we check the fibre type, condition and problem areas.
2. **Vacuum:** we remove loose dry soil first.
3. **Pre-treatment:** we apply a solution matched to your carpet to break down dirt and grease.
4. **Agitation:** we work the solution gently into the pile.
5. **Hot water extraction:** we rinse and extract, removing dirt and most of the moisture.
6. **Grooming:** we reset the pile so it dries evenly and looks its best.

## Benefits of professional carpet cleaning

- Removes allergens, dust mites and bacteria
- Restores colour and softness
- Gets rid of trapped odours
- Extends the life of your carpets

## How long will my carpets take to dry?

Most carpets are dry within 2 to 4 hours, depending on the carpet type, ventilation and the weather.
