## Stop Carpet Moths Before They Spread

You've chosen your carpets, rugs and clothes with care, and carpet moths can ruin them quietly in a matter of months. Their larvae feed on keratin, the protein in wool and other natural fibres, leaving bald patches, holes and thinning pile behind.

We find where they're hiding, treat every stage of their life cycle and help you keep them from coming back, so your home and your wardrobe stay protected.

## Signs of Carpet Moths

- Bald or thinning patches, especially along skirting boards and under furniture
- Small cream-coloured larvae or silky cases in the pile
- Small golden-brown moths that crawl rather than fly
- Holes appearing in wool or cashmere clothes

## Who's Most at Risk?

Carpet moths are a common household pest across the UK, and are especially widespread in the South and West of England. Homes with wool carpets, natural-fibre rugs and wardrobes full of wool or cashmere are most at risk. Left untreated, an infestation can spread from your carpets into drawers and wardrobes.
