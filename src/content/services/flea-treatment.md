## Fleas Gone, Comfort Restored

You want your home to be a comfortable place for your family and your pets, not a source of itchy bites. Fleas live and breed in carpets, rugs and soft furnishings, and they spread quickly once they've moved in.

We treat every area your pets use, break the flea life cycle and give you a 30-day revisit guarantee, so you can relax at home again.

## Why Fleas Need Professional Treatment

- **They bite people as well as pets.** Bites cause itching, irritation and a red rash.
- **They affect your pets' health.** Some cats and dogs develop allergies to flea saliva.
- **They hide deep in your carpets.** Eggs and larvae sit at the base of the pile, out of reach of household sprays.
- **They can outlast your pets.** Fleas can survive in a home long after the animals that brought them have gone.
