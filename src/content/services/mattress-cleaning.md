## A Fresher Mattress for a Better Night's Sleep

You spend around a third of your life in bed, and your mattress quietly collects sweat, dead skin, dust mites and spills along the way. You deserve a bed that feels clean, smells fresh and helps you sleep well.

Our mattress cleaning removes stains, allergens and odours, and can save a good mattress from a premature trip to the tip.

## How We Clean Your Mattress

1. **We inspect it:** we look at the materials, stains and odours, so we can plan the right approach.
2. **We vacuum it:** an industrial vacuum lifts loose dust, skin and debris.
3. **We pre-treat it:** we choose cleaning solutions to suit your mattress and its stains.
4. **We deep clean it:** hot water extraction rinses deep into the fibres while powerful suction lifts the dirt straight back out.

## Benefits of Hot Water Extraction

- Removes most allergens and dust mites
- Kills most bacteria
- Lifts stains and stubborn odours
- Uses eco, pet and child-friendly solutions
- Helps you get a better night's sleep
