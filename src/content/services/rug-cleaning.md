## Rugs need specialist care

Rugs are often made from natural fibres and dyes that need a gentler approach than fitted carpets. We test for colour fastness and pick a method that suits the material.

## Rugs we clean

- Wool and wool-blend rugs
- Synthetic rugs
- Shaggy and deep pile rugs
- Hall runners
